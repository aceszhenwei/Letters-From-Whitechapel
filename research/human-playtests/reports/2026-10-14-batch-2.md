## Defect candidates

None.

- **Both arrests follow v3's rule.** They were at belief ≥ 0.2 (0.225 and 1.0); there were no forced arrests, and no disagreement with the script's model.
- **No sign of hidden information:** Jack's true circle never had zero belief (0 of 529 decisions).
- **The out-of-moves loss** is the rules working: Jack had no legal way home in time.

## Recommendation

**No new investigation.** No defect candidate; the one recurring pattern (the diffuse belief) has already been investigated twice; and no game is alarming.
- **The outstanding proposal stands:** the offline calibration check of v3's position belief on human records, proposed by the [coach investigation](2026-10-11-investigation-coaches.md) and narrowed by the [movement study](2026-10-11-movement.md). This batch's weaker gap makes it more useful, not less, because it would show whether the gap depends on the player.
- **A cumulative review is due at about 20 games** (12 now).

## Limitations

- **Five games in two cohorts** (3 and 2). The cohorts play identically but are kept apart, so per-cohort figures rest on 2–3 games.
- **Players are anonymous; experience isn't recorded.** New hideouts suggest, but don't show, new players.
- **The comparisons with earlier games are descriptive.** They pool across 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 only in the "This batch" column, where noted.
