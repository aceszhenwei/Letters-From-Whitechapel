The first formal release of *Letters From Whitechapel*: a browser version of the board game, complete over four nights, playable as the detectives or as Jack the Ripper.

**▶ Play online:** https://aceszhenwei.github.io/Letters-From-Whitechapel/ (nothing to install; works on computers and phones)

## The game

- **All four nights** of the revised edition's standard rules, including:
  - the double event;
  - real and fake patrols;
  - the Time of the Crime, with Jack waiting and the Wretched moving;
  - coaches and alleys;
  - searching for clues and making arrests.
- **A verified map:** the board was checked against the physical game.
- **Rules not implemented** (see [Game rules](https://github.com/aceszhenwei/Letters-From-Whitechapel/blob/v1.0.0/docs/game-rules.md#not-implemented)): the optional rules and the Head of the Investigation tiles.

## Two ways to play

| You play | Your opponent | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **The detectives** (default) | The computer's Jack | **Normal**: Strategic Jack (default) · **Hard**: Jack AI v2, who hides the way to his hideout |
| **Jack the Ripper** | The computer's five detectives | **Easy**: Detective AI v2 · **Normal**: Detective AI v3 (default), who also prepares for the last night |

- **Playing Jack:** choose a secret hideout, place the women, kill or wait, then walk, take a coach or slip through an alley back home before your moves run out. The detectives' turns are paced so you can follow them, and you can skip ahead.
- **Fair opponents:** the computer's detectives see only what detectives at the table would. Tests check that their moves never depend on Jack's hidden hideout or route.
- **Saved preferences:** each role remembers its own difficulty.

## On any screen

- **Phones:** zoom and pan the board, tap targets sized for fingers, and (playing Jack) the buttons repeated under the board.
- **For the detectives:**
  - undo a policeman's move until you confirm;
  - review each night as the police saw it, with walking distances from crime scenes and clues;
  - search a policeman's remaining circles, or every policeman's, in one tap.

## Game records

- **Export:** save any game as a versioned JSON file:
  - the police's record at any time;
  - Jack's record when you play him;
  - the full record once the game is over.
- **Offline tools:** `npm run research:import` validates, replays and summarises exported games. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## For developers and researchers

- **Developer Mode** (`index.html?dev=1`): every AI level, an experimental Jack with strategic waiting, and computer police to watch.
- **Headless engine:** the rules, engine and AIs run in Node without a page. Seeded simulations and test tiers (fast, smoke, medium, full) are included, with 280 automated tests.
- **Research reports** behind every AI ([documentation index](https://github.com/aceszhenwei/Letters-From-Whitechapel/blob/v1.0.0/docs/README.md#research-reports)):
  - pre-registered where it mattered;
  - with paired seeds and held-out validation;
  - with negative results recorded.
