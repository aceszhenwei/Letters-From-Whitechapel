      London, 1888

      Letters From Whitechapel

      Jack the Ripper is loose in Whitechapel. Over four nights he will kill and slip back to his secret hideout, while five detectives try to catch him.

      Who do you play?

      • Place your patrols, then watch where the bodies are found.
      • Move your policemen up to two crossings and search nearby circles for clues.
      • When you know where Jack is, make the arrest.
      • Choose a secret hideout, then place the women and choose your victim.
      • After each murder, walk back to your hideout before your moves run out, using coaches and alleys to slip past the police.
      • The computer's detectives see only what detectives at the table would: never your hideout or your route.
